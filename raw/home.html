HOW A SMART HOME ACTUALLY WORKS

A hub sits in the middle and talks to everything else. Sensors report what is happening — someone at the door, water on the floor, a room that has emptied. The hub decides what should follow, and lights, locks, curtains or cameras respond.

The hub is the one purchase worth thinking about first, because it decides what you can add later. We will tell you which one suits your home before you spend anything.

01

One App, Total Control

Lights, locks, curtains, cameras and sensors in one app — and in Apple Home, Google Home or Alexa if your household already uses one. No separate app per brand.

02

Wiring, or no wiring

Sensors, locks and battery devices need no wiring at all. Wired switches need a neutral wire at the switchboard, which many Indian homes do not have — send us a photo and we will tell you before you order.

03

Voice Ready

Works with Apple Home, Google Home and Alexa. Matter devices can be shared across all three at once, so a mixed household is not a problem.

04

Built for Indian conditions

Concrete walls cut wireless range. Power cuts interrupt anything cloud-dependent. Monsoon humidity affects adhesives and sensors. We plan around all three rather than assuming the conditions these products were designed for.

  • DOOR LOCK 35–55mm The door thickness most smart locks accept — the Aqara U100's own published range. A solid teak door here is often 60 mm or more. We measure yours before we quote, because a lock that will not close is a return, not an installation.
  • MOTION SENSOR 0detections once you sit still A PIR sensor sees you cross between its detection zones. Sit still to read and your heat stops moving between zones, so it decides the room is empty and switches the light off. For a living room we fit mmWave presence, which reads breathing.
  • GAS DETECTOR 10–15cm LPG is heavier than air — molecular weight 44 against air's 28.9 — so it pools at floor level. A detector mounted near the ceiling may never trigger. Ours go low, near the cylinder, where the gas actually collects.

Figures are the manufacturers' and the standards' own, not ours: Aqara publishes 35–55 mm door thickness for the Smart Lock U100; propane's vapour density puts LPG detectors 10–15 cm off the floor while methane detectors go near the ceiling; PIR sensors detect movement between zones and time out on a still occupant, which is the limitation mmWave exists to solve.

THE STANDARD UNDERNEATH

What is Matter in smart home automation?

Matter is not a radio — it is a common language that lets devices from different brands work together across Apple Home, Google Home and Alexa at the same time. Before Matter, a device usually belonged to one ecosystem and stayed there.

In practice it means you are not locked into one brand for the next decade.
